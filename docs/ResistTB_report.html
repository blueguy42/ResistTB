ResistTB: progress report

GenoHack 2026 · primer and probe design for drug-resistant TB · status 25 October 2026, about 01:30 WIB · in silico only

Nothing has been tested in a tube. All coverage figures are measured on the same 456 isolates the oligos were designed on, so they are optimistic. Held-out and wet-lab validation have not been done.

What it is

ResistTB (formerly ChatGP-TB) turns public Mycobacterium tuberculosis genomes into qPCR primers and allele-specific probes for drug-resistance variants. A cloud VM called variants per isolate (snippy, FreeBayes, TB-Profiler) [8][9][11]. A Python library designs oligos on the population consensus with Primer3 [16], checks them against every isolate (overall, by lineage, by region), screens off-target amplification with BLAST [17], scores them and tests tube sharing. A Gemini agent on Vertex AI [19] turns free text into a design call. A web interface shows ranked assays and a library of 205 WHO resistance variants [5]. It is live at https://resist-tb.afanhandoyo.com.

What the pooled rebuild showed

  • rpoB S450L fell from 83 (Bandung) to 61, then recovered to 80. At the strict 2% variable-site tolerance Primer3 offered only 20 near-identical pairs, all with 4–6 near-matches in H37Rv. Trying looser tolerances restored the original pair. Five rpoB designs sharing it gained 18–22 points; only three other designs changed (rrl +2, rrs −1, ethA −2).
  • Tamil Nadu and lineage 1 are weakest for rpoB S450L (23/61 exact; lineage 1 only 26/58). The cause is mostly one uncalled base at position 761152 inside the probe in 39 isolates, with no variant in the VCF: a coverage dropout, not a polymorphism, though this was not confirmed from raw reads. The tool counts an uncalled base as a mismatch, so Tamil Nadu coverage is probably underestimated.
  • Library: 192 of 205 designed (13 not: 10 rpoB RRDR, 2 pncA, katG S315R). 161 designs score 80 or more; 129 have specificity 100; 20 still score 0 (BLAST found off-target products or near-matches). The 109-pair BLAST cache found 85 clean pairs, a few with non-tuberculous mycobacteria products and one with 43 predicted human products.
  • Isolate QC: median depth 83x (Bandung), 91x (Guangdong), 56x (Tamil Nadu); median 98.6–99.5% reads mapped. Four isolates look contaminated or mixed (19–49% mapped) and are still in the design.

How to validate readiness and regional suitability

  • Regional suitability (no lab): leave-one-region-out coverage (design without one region, score it) and held-out coverage on the 779 other Indonesian runs, with Wilson 95% intervals; a per-lineage floor; separate uncalled from true mismatches; report coverage under more than one 3′-mismatch rule, because that rule changes the estimate [31][33].
  • Readiness (lab): MIQE-style metrics [34]: efficiency (typically 90–110%), dynamic range, limit of detection, inclusivity and exclusivity panels, allele discrimination. Then sensitivity and specificity against phenotypic DST with sequencing for discordant results, benchmarked on Xpert MTB/XDR (about 96% isoniazid and 94% fluoroquinolone sensitivity in Anhui; 99.2% and 88.4% in Georgia) [35][36] and the WHO target product profiles (2024) [37]. The numeric acceptance targets are from memory, not from a cited source.
  • Caution: in silico specificity predicted wet-lab results poorly in an eDNA study (BLAST predicted about 67% of outcomes) [32].

Limitations

  • In silico only; coverage is in-sample; the score weights and most thresholds are heuristics without a published source.
  • Region labels are per BioProject; Tamil Nadu is small (61) and lineage-1 heavy; four low-quality isolates are included; uncalled bases count as mismatches.
  • Specificity is a BLAST screen with a pair-keyed cache; 20 designs score 0; the multiplex check covers dimers only and assumes 4 channels.
  • Only near-fixed variant calls (no heteroresistance); 13 variants not designed (dense rpoB, pncA).
  • Not a diagnostic; no clinical advice. The public endpoint has per-instance in-memory rate limits; Gemini cost per chat is unmeasured; domain mapping is a preview feature.
