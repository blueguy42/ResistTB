ResistTB: how the system works

Genomics-guided primer and probe design for drug-resistant tuberculosis. GenoHack 2026 prototype. All results are in silico.

The system is ResistTB: it turns 456 real TB genomes from three regions into qPCR primers and probes for drug-resistant TB, and explains the result through a chat interface and a browsable library. It is live at resist-tb.afanhandoyo.com. Updated 25 October 2026.

Input

  • Genomes: 456 M. tuberculosis isolates from public data: Bandung, Indonesia (222), Guangdong, China (173) and Tamil Nadu, India (61), plus the H37Rv reference genome, its gene annotation, and a mask of unreliable regions.
  • Evidence and targets: the WHO 2023 mutation catalogue (which mutations cause resistance, and how strong the evidence is). It also supplies the library of 205 designable resistance variants.
  • Off-target genomes: human, 7 non-tuberculous mycobacteria and 6 respiratory bacteria, used to check for cross-reaction.
  • A request: a chat message such as "design an assay for isoniazid resistance", or up to 6 variants picked in the target library.

Output

A ranked report of the assays requested (192 of the 205 library variants have a design; the four headline assays are inhA promoter, gyrA, katG and rpoB). Each assay has:

  • a primer pair
  • a wild-type probe and a mutant probe, with their sequences, positions and melting temperatures
  • four scores
  • coverage across the 456 isolates, overall, by region and by lineage
  • the predicted off-target result
  • which assays can share a tube
  • the caveats

Everything is in silico. Nothing has been tested in a tube.

Things to know for the proposal

  • Evidence strength. Each assay carries its own limits in the report: in silico only, coverage measured on the same isolates the assay was designed on, and the probe discrimination as an estimate.
  • Targets. Four targets (rpoB S450L, katG S315T, inhA c.-15, gyrA D94G) were hand-built; the other 201 are generated from the WHO catalogue (single-base changes seen in at least 10 resistant isolates). 13 catalogue variants in dense or indel-rich sites cannot be designed and are explained instead.
